# 01 — Global Design Bible

The single source of truth for tone, scope, and the non-negotiable rules of the project. Every other
document, and every line of mod code, must be consistent with this one.

## 1. Premise

The world of **2069** is the wreckage of the twentieth-century international order, rebuilt into
something new. Ecological collapse, the corporate wars, economic fragmentation, and the destruction
of the global Net (the DataKrash) broke the old system but did not end history. States survived.
Corporations grew into geopolitical actors rivaling states. A new, fractured Net is being rebuilt
under contested control.

The central question of the mod: **does the future belong to national governments, supranational
blocs, corporate empires, or autonomous networks?**

## 2. Scope and framing

- This is a **global geopolitical total conversion**. The unit of play is the world order, not a
  city.
- **Night City** is a major strategic flashpoint — one of several — not the narrative center.
- The character **V does not appear** in any form. No 2077-protagonist storyline.
- The **Unification War** is one theater of a broader international confrontation, not the spine of
  the campaign.

## 3. The independent power centers (the core rule)

> The NUSA, Neo-Soviet Union, Europe, Japan, China, and the major corporations are independent
> centers of power. No single North American storyline defines the global campaign.

Six centers of gravity, each with its own institutions, economy, ideology space, and ambitions:

1. **New United States of America (NUSA)** — recovering continental power.
2. **Neo-Soviet Union** — first-tier Eurasian superpower.
3. **Europe** — dense, divided region of states + institutions + corporations.
4. **Arasaka & Japan** — global corporation *and* sovereign state, kept distinct.
5. **China & Kang Tao** — rising Pacific power + weapons-tech corporation.
6. **Transnational corporations** — Arasaka, Militech, SovOil, Petrochem, Kang Tao, Biotechnica,
   Orbital Air, Euro Business Machines, and others as geopolitical agents.

### The Neo-Soviet rule (non-negotiable)

> The Neo-Soviet Union must never be treated as a minor regional faction or merely as the political
> shell of SovOil. It is a major sovereign superpower with its own institutions, ideological
> possibilities, military strategy, economic systems, and global ambitions.

The Neo-Soviet focus tree is one of the **largest and most developed** in the mod, comparable to the
NUSA and to Europe as a whole. Its design document (`08`) sits immediately after the NUSA (`07`),
ahead of any regional faction.

## 4. The five global conflict axes

The mod is deliberately **not** reducible to "Militech versus Arasaka." Overlapping struggles:

| Axis | Principal actors | Central issue |
|------|------------------|---------------|
| **Atlantic** | NUSA, Britain, Western Europe, Militech, Atlantic corps | Can Atlantic cooperation be restored, and under whose leadership? |
| **Eurasian** | Neo-Soviet Union, E/C Europe, SovOil, China, client states | Control of energy, transport, industrial corridors, alignment. |
| **European** | EEC institutions, national governments, corporations, movements, NUSA & Neo-Soviet influence | Federation, sovereignty, corporate capture, or fragmentation? |
| **Pacific** | Japan, Arasaka, China, Kang Tao, Pacific states, NUSA allies | Strategic and economic control of the Pacific. |
| **Corporate** | Arasaka, Militech, SovOil, Petrochem, Kang Tao, Biotechnica, Orbital Air, EBM | Do corporations remain instruments of states or become sovereign powers? |

## 5. Tone

- **Grounded near-future geopolitics**, not cyberpunk pastiche. Chrome and neon are texture, not
  substance. The drama is institutional: who governs, who owns, who mobilizes.
- **Moral ambiguity by default.** Every principal power has a democratic/reformist path and an
  authoritarian/corporate-capture path. The mod does not pre-judge the winner.
- **Systems tell the story.** Prefer expressing themes through mechanics (cohesion, corporate
  capture, integration) over scripted narration.

## 6. The flagship campaign: "The Fractured World, 2069"

Four interconnected campaigns, none subordinate to another:

1. **Reunification of America** — NUSA, Militech, Free States, Texas, Night City, Arasaka.
2. **The European Crisis** — EEC institutions, Germany, France, Britain, corporate interests, foreign
   influence.
3. **The Eurasian Settlement** — Neo-Soviet Union, SovOil, constituent republics, Eastern Europe,
   China, European competition.
4. **The Pacific Balance** — Japan, Arasaka, China, Kang Tao, NUSA interests.

## 7. What this mod is not

- Not a Night City map mod.
- Not a V / 2077 story mod.
- Not "USA vs corporations" with everyone else as backdrop.
- Not a Russia flavor pack where SovOil is the real government.

## 8. Glossary (anchor terms)

- **NUSA** — New United States of America.
- **DataKrash** — the event that destroyed the old global Net.
- **The new Net** — the rebuilt, fragmented, contested network infrastructure.
- **EEC** — European Economic Community; supranational framework, not a single country.
- **Union cohesion** — Neo-Soviet mechanic measuring Moscow's ability to mobilize the federation.
- **Corporate sovereignty** — the degree to which a corporation acts as, or captures, a state.
