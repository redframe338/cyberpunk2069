# 05 — Global Economic System

> **Status: Outline.** Deepened alongside Phases 2–3 (`14`).

## 1. Purpose

Define the shared economic layer so that each power's economic identity (`04`) sits on common rules:
resources, energy, food, the new Net, and corporate ownership.

## 2. Strategic resources (proposed reframing of HOI4 resources)

- **Energy** (oil / CHOOH2 / power) — the master strategic resource; SovOil, Petrochem leverage.
- **Food & water** — scarcity as a national pressure (NUSA, others).
- **Rare materials / electronics** — for cyber-military and the new Net.
- **Industrial capacity** — reconstruction-limited for post-collapse states.

## 3. Cross-cutting economic mechanics

- **Energy dependency modifier** — states reliant on SovOil/Petrochem energy carry a leverage
  vulnerability (ties `08` §2.4 to `09`).
- **Corporate ownership share** — how much of a state's economy corporations control (feeds `06`).
- **Reconstruction level** — post-collapse states rebuild industry over time.
- **Consumer-goods / scarcity pressure** — stability cost of misallocation.

## 4. Per-power economic identity (see `04` for the matrix)

Each Tier One power expresses identity through laws + national spirits, not a bespoke UI unless
justified (the Neo-Soviet planning↔market system in `08` is the main bespoke case).

## 5. Open questions

- How much to reuse vanilla economy laws vs. custom.
- Whether energy is a resource, a variable, or both.
- Global trade model: vanilla market vs. restricted post-collapse trade.
