# 09 — European System Design

> **Status: Outline.** EEC framework prototype in Phase 2; full European systems in Phase 3 (`14`).
>
> **MAP DECISION (user):** At the 2069 start the EEC is a **single unified country** (`EEC`) owning all
> of continental Europe; the **UK, Turkey, and USSR are independent** (`02` D2, `03` §5.2). The
> "competing member states" material below is therefore reframed as **internal politics of the EEC**
> (factions/paths within one country) rather than separate playable nations — unless a later design
> reintroduces breakup/release mechanics for members.

## 1. Identity

Europe is **not one generic country.** It is a dense political region of competing states,
institutions, currencies, corporations, and security arrangements. It should be economically advanced
but politically divided.

## 2. Central question

**The nature of integration** — federation, sovereignty, corporate capture, or fragmentation.

## 3. Actors

Per the canon map, the **ECC** is a cohesive continental bloc, broader than a Western-Europe core:

- **ECC member states:** Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Benelux, Denmark, Ireland, **Poland,
  Czechoslovakia, Austria, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Yugoslavia, Greece**.
- **ECC associates:** Norway, Sweden, Finland, Iceland, Greenland.
- **Independent (non-ECC):** the **United Kingdom** (outside the bloc — a key Atlantic-axis pivot),
  Turkey.
- Institutions: the **European Economic Community (EEC)** overlay and the **European Space Agency (ESA)**.
- Corporations: **Euro Business Machines (EBM)**, regional banking/corporate interests, Biotechnica.

Because canon Europe already includes the East, the integration struggle runs *within* a large existing
bloc (deepen vs. fracture) as much as it is about who joins. Full tag list in `03` §2.2.

## 4. The EEC framework (shared mechanic, not a conventional country)

A supranational overlay carrying shared mechanics across European states: common institutions, a
currency question, integration level, and collective decisions. Represented as an overlay
faction + shared national ideas rather than a tag owning territory (finalize in `03`).

**Integration level** rises or falls via member-state focuses and events, unlocking (or dissolving)
common policy.

## 5. Possible European outcomes

Deeper federal integration · sovereign national restoration · corporate technocracy · social-democratic
reconstruction · authoritarian fortress Europe · fragmentation into competing regional blocs ·
accommodation with the Neo-Soviet Union · renewed Atlantic alignment with the NUSA.

## 6. External pressure

Europe is contested by **NUSA influence** (Atlantic axis) and **Neo-Soviet influence + energy
leverage** (Eurasian axis, `08` §2.4). The energy-dependency modifier (`05`) is a key lever.

## 7. Launch scope

Phase 2: one major European state (or the EEC framework) as a vertical slice. Phase 3: full national
sovereignty paths, integration system, and corporate-influence layer for the major states.

## 8. Open questions

- Which single European power leads the Phase 2 slice (Germany vs. France vs. EEC framework).
- Currency mechanic depth.
- How ESA ties into the space/tech layer (`13`).
## Biotechnica integrated authority

Biotechnica is represented by playable tag `BIO` as the **Biotechnica European Bioengineering
Authority**. It is a directly integrated scientific corporation under the EEC, not a politically
independent corporate power. The EEC retains cores on all ten Biotechnica-administered research,
agricultural, pharmaceutical, restoration, and export territories.

Its prototype gameplay loop tracks `biotechnica_research_progress` from Research Foundation to
Bioeconomic Superpower. Biotechnica strengthens civilian industry, food security, pharmaceuticals,
infrastructure, environmental recovery, and scientific research. It has no normal independence path
and only a small defensive security force. This distinguishes it from Arasaka's political subversion,
Militech's military-industrial power, and SovOil's energy logistics.
