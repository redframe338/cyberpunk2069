# 10 — Japan and Arasaka Design

> **Status: Outline.** Both playable in the Phase 2 vertical slice (`14`).

## 1. Core principle

**Arasaka is a global actor but does not replace Japan.** Keep distinct:

- the **Japanese state**,
- **Arasaka** (the corporation),
- Japanese **political factions**,
- **other Japanese corporations**,
- the **Arasaka family's** internal struggle.

## 2. Arasaka — identity and goals

A global security-tech corporation on the corporate sovereignty ladder (`06`), start position
**Parallel power** within Japan. Strategic goals:

- Restoring its North American presence (Phase 4 hook, Atlantic/Pacific axes).
- Dominating Japanese politics.
- Securing European markets.
- Countering **Militech**.
- Accessing Eurasian energy.
- Controlling digital-identity and consciousness technologies (`13`).
- Building a global security architecture.

Internal layer: the **Arasaka family struggle** as a leadership/faction mechanic.

## 3. Japan — possible paths

- Alignment with Arasaka (corporate-capture end).
- Reassertion of civilian state control.
- Corporate pluralism (balance Arasaka against other corps).
- Strategic alignment with Europe.
- Pacific security cooperation.
- Anti-corporate reform.

Central question: **civilian state control or Arasaka capture?**

## 4. Unique mechanic

The Japan↔Arasaka corporate sovereignty ladder (`06`) is the shared spine, expressed from both sides:
Japan pushes the ladder *down* (reassert control), Arasaka pushes it *up* (toward sovereign
corporation). Playing either tag contests the same ladder from opposite ends.

## 5. Pacific axis

Japan and Arasaka sit at the center of the Pacific struggle with China, Kang Tao, Pacific states, and
NUSA allies (`01` §4, `11`).

## 6. Open questions

- Whether Arasaka is a full country tag from launch or emerges via the ladder.
- Representation of other Japanese corporations.
- Consciousness-tech (Soulkiller / Secure Your Soul) as a tech-tree element vs. flavor (`13`).
