# 13 — Technology and Warfare

> **Status: Outline.** Stubbed in Phase 2, deepened alongside Phases 3–4 (`14`).

## 1. Purpose

Reframe HOI4's research and combat for the 2069 setting without losing the strategy game's core.

## 2. Technology domains (proposed research trees)

- **Land / mechanized / powered infantry** — cyber-augmented ground forces, drones.
- **Air & orbital** — aerospace, orbital logistics (Orbital Air, ESA), limited space assets.
- **Naval** — reconstruction-limited; regional power projection.
- **Cyber-electronic warfare** — attack/defend the new Net; interacts with `12`.
- **The new Net** — infrastructure control as a tech + strategic layer (who governs the rebuilt Net).
- **Consciousness / biotech** — Arasaka consciousness tech, Biotechnica bio/agri; flavor-to-mechanic
  bridge, kept restrained per tone (`01`).
- **Industrial reconstruction** — post-collapse rebuild multipliers (`05`).

## 3. Warfare identity by power

- **Neo-Soviet:** mass mobilization + deep battle + strong missile/space + cyber layer (`08` §4),
  gated by cohesion.
- **NUSA / Militech:** mechanized land power + PMC integration.
- **Arasaka:** elite security forces, cyber, consciousness tech.
- **China / Kang Tao:** smart-weapons emphasis.

## 4. The new Net as a strategic layer

Control of the rebuilt Net is contested (Cybernetic Socialism `08` §3.2, EBM `09`, Arasaka `10`).
Consider a shared "Net governance" mechanic feeding cyber-ops effectiveness (`12`).

## 5. Doctrines

Map vanilla doctrine slots to 2069 equivalents; add a cyber-electronic doctrine axis.

## 6. Open questions

- How prominent orbital/space warfare is at launch vs. later.
- Whether consciousness tech grants concrete bonuses or stays largely narrative.
- Degree of divergence from vanilla equipment archetypes.
