# 04 — Major Power Matrix

A one-page comparison of every launch-content power: its identity, unique mechanic, economic model,
principal conflict axes, and internal fault line. This is the quick-reference that keeps the powers
**structurally distinct** from one another.

## Tier One — principal powers (full launch content)

| Power | Identity | Unique mechanic | Economic model | Primary axes | Central internal question |
|-------|----------|-----------------|----------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| **NUSA** | Recovering continental state | Reunification / federal authority | State-directed reconstruction | Atlantic, Corporate | Democracy or militarized corporate rule? |
| **Neo-Soviet Union** | First-tier Eurasian superpower | **Union cohesion** + SovOil relationship | Planning ↔ market spectrum | Eurasian, European | Socialist federation, security state, energy empire, or SovOil-captured? |
| **Germany (or C-Euro lead)** | Industrial heart of Europe | European integration leverage | Social-market / technocratic | European, Atlantic | Federal Europe or sovereign nation? |
| **France** | Political core of Europe | Strategic autonomy / force projection | Dirigiste | European, Atlantic | Federation, sovereignty, or fortress Europe? |
| **United Kingdom** | Atlantic pivot | Atlantic alignment vs. autonomy | Financialized | Atlantic, European | Rejoin Europe, ally the NUSA, or go it alone? |
| **Japan** | Sovereign Pacific state | State vs. corporate control | Advanced export economy | Pacific | Civilian state control or Arasaka capture? |
| **Arasaka** | Global security-tech corporation | Corporate sovereignty ladder | Corporate conglomerate | Pacific, Atlantic, Corporate | Instrument of Japan or sovereign power? |
| **China** | Rising Pacific–Eurasian power | State–corporate fusion | State capitalism | Pacific, Eurasian | Reform, hardline, or corporate-fusion state? |
| **Militech** | Arms-and-industry corporation | Corporate sovereignty ladder | Military-industrial | Atlantic, Corporate | NUSA's arsenal or independent power? |
| **MetaCorp Technologies** | Civilian technology corporation | Federal technology influence | Computing, semiconductors, robotics, digital infrastructure | Corporate, Atlantic | National technology partner, regulated utility, or digital state? |

The **EEC** is not a conventional playable country but carries **major shared mechanics** across
European powers (integration, common institutions, currency) — see `09`.

## Tier Three — secondary states and corporate actors

Generic systems, regional content, or later expansions. Everything not listed above.

## Distinctiveness checklist

No two Tier One powers may share **all** of: unique mechanic, economic model, and internal question.
Before adding content to a power, confirm it advances that power's *own* column above. Corporate
powers (Arasaka, Militech) share the corporate-sovereignty ladder but must differ in economic base
and axes.
