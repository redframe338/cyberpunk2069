# 17 — MetaCorp Technologies Design

MetaCorp Technologies (`MTC`) is the NUSA's premier civilian technology,
artificial-intelligence, semiconductor, robotics, telecommunications, computing,
and cybernetics corporation. It begins as a playable **Strategic Technology
Corporation** under NUSA sovereignty.

## Territorial Structure

Suggested technology enclaves:

- MetaCorp Global Headquarters
- Silicon Research Campus
- National Semiconductor Manufacturing Center
- Artificial Intelligence Development Institute
- Robotics Manufacturing Complex
- Continental Data Center Network
- Cybernetics Research Campus
- Telecommunications Operations Center
- Quantum Computing Laboratory
- Consumer Electronics Manufacturing Zone

## Territorial Design Requirements

MetaCorp should **not** control large continuous regions of the NUSA.

Instead, it should directly administer a network of **small, strategically placed
corporate enclaves** representing technology campuses, semiconductor fabrication
plants, AI research facilities, robotics manufacturing centers, data centers,
logistics hubs, and secure corporate districts.

Each MetaCorp-controlled state should be compact and densely developed rather
than geographically large.

MetaCorp must begin the game with **at least one coastal enclave** providing
direct access to the sea.

This coastal territory should represent a major corporate port used for importing
rare materials, exporting advanced electronics, international logistics,
container shipping, overseas technology trade, and corporate naval logistics.

The coastal enclave should include a naval base, high infrastructure, a supply
hub, dockyard capacity where appropriate, industrial facilities, and strong
coastal defenses.

The remaining MetaCorp territories should be distributed across major technology
corridors within the NUSA rather than clustered into one large area. Examples
include Silicon Valley-style research districts, semiconductor fabrication
campuses, AI supercomputing centers, robotics manufacturing facilities, national
telecommunications headquarters, and cloud-computing and data-center complexes.

Whenever possible, use existing small states or create appropriately sized new
states from existing provinces. Do **not** assign MetaCorp ownership of entire
large states simply to represent one corporate facility.

The NUSA must retain cores on every MetaCorp-controlled territory. MetaCorp
should receive only the territory necessary to represent its strategic corporate
infrastructure while maintaining high industrial output, exceptional research
capability, and strong economic value despite its relatively small geographic
footprint.
