# 12 — Global Diplomacy Design

> **Status: Outline.** Stubbed in Phase 2, deepened alongside Phases 3–4 (`14`).

## 1. Purpose

The cross-cutting systems by which powers compete short of open war: influence, energy leverage,
intelligence, and the new Net. This is where the five conflict axes (`01` §4) are mechanically fought.

## 2. Influence system

A shared framework (the Neo-Soviet Eurasian-influence engine in `08` §2.4 is the reference
implementation) for contesting third-party states. Tools:

- energy agreements, military guarantees, infrastructure construction, political support, debt
  arrangements, intelligence operations, cyber operations, direct intervention.

Applied by the NUSA (Atlantic/Latin America), Neo-Soviet Union (Eurasia), China (Pacific/Eurasia),
Europe (internally), and corporations (`06`).

## 3. Energy leverage

The **energy-dependency modifier** (`05`) as a diplomatic weapon — SovOil/Petrochem over dependent
states, especially Europe. Cutting or guaranteeing energy is a coercive lever.

## 4. Intelligence and cyber operations

Operations that can attack Neo-Soviet **union cohesion** (`08`), NUSA **federal authority** (`07`),
European **integration** (`09`), and corporate ladders (`06`). Ties to control of the new Net (`13`).

## 5. Factions and blocs

The mod supports multiple rival alliance systems, not a single Allies/Axis binary:

- Atlantic alignment (NUSA-led), Eurasian bloc (Neo-Soviet-led), a European framework (EEC),
  Pacific arrangements, and a corporate/sovereign-corp bloc.

## 6. Open questions

- How many distinct faction mechanics vs. reused vanilla alliances.
- Whether influence is a variable, a decision economy, or both.
- Interaction ordering between influence, energy leverage, and cyber ops.
