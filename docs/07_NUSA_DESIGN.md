# 07 — NUSA Design

> **Status: Outline.** Vertical-slice content in Phase 2; full content in Phase 4 (`14`).

## 1. Identity

The **New United States of America** — a recovering continental power reconstructing state authority
after decades of fragmentation. **Powerful but internally compromised.**

## 2. Central question

Does federal restoration mean **democracy** or **militarized corporate rule**?

## 3. Strategic problems (focus-tree pillars)

- Reunifying the former United States — canon reunification targets are the **Western Corporate States
  (WCS)**, the **Free States**, and the **Republic of Texas** (Canada is a separate strong power, not a
  target). See `03` §5.1.
- Managing **Night City** as an internal **contested flashpoint**: Southern California (incl. Night
  City) is NUSA territory at start, but a scripted crisis can see it break away to independence or to
  **Arasaka** (tag `NCT`; `02` D3, `03` §5.0). A core interlock with the Pacific/corporate axes.
- Controlling **Militech** (corporate sovereignty ladder, `06`).
- Rebuilding industry.
- Securing food and energy (`05`).
- Containing internal instability.
- Competing with **Arasaka** (Atlantic + Pacific axes).
- Restoring influence in Latin America.

## 4. Unique mechanic — Federal Authority / Reunification

A system tracking Washington's reach over the reintegrating states and the terms of reunion
(negotiated vs. coerced). Interacts with the Militech ladder and with the democracy↔corporate-rule
question. (Design counterpart to Neo-Soviet **union cohesion**, but distinct in mechanics — negotiated
reintegration of near-sovereign states vs. holding an existing federation together.)

## 5. Political paths (to be detailed)

- Democratic federal restoration.
- Militarized / security-state restoration.
- Corporate-managed union (Militech-captured).
- Others TBD.

## 6. Military identity

Militech-supplied land power; PMC integration; reconstruction-limited navy/air. Detail in Phase 4.

## 7. Open questions

- Relationship between Federal Authority and the corporate sovereignty ladder for Militech.
- Which reunification targets are diplomatic vs. military at start.

## 8. Implemented Militech prototype

Militech is represented by playable tag `MLT` as a Strategic Defense Corporation under the NUSA.
It administers ten dispersed defense enclaves rather than a continuous territorial state. The NUSA
retains cores on every enclave. Its prototype includes a federal-influence variable, six influence
tiers, bilateral decisions, crisis events, elite security formations, corporate state modifiers, and
cooperation, independence, military-government, and nationalization outcomes.

Militech's gameplay identity is conventional military power: armor, aerospace, logistics, weapons
production, federal procurement, and defense mobilization. It should not inherit Arasaka's espionage
identity or SovOil's energy-management identity.

## 9. Implemented MetaCorp prototype

MetaCorp Technologies is represented by playable tag `MTC` as a Strategic Technology Corporation
under the NUSA. It administers ten one-province technology enclaves distributed across existing
NUSA territory, with the NUSA retaining cores on every enclave. MetaCorp supplies civilian
computing, semiconductors, artificial intelligence, robotics, telecommunications, cybernetics,
cloud infrastructure, and advanced electronics.

MetaCorp and Militech form competing federal corporate pillars. NUSA decisions can fund, regulate,
or balance them; investment in one generally reduces the other's federal influence. See `17`.
