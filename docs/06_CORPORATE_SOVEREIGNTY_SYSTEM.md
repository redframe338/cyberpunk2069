# 06 — Corporate Sovereignty System

> **Status: Outline.** Prototype with Arasaka in Phase 2; generalized in Phases 3–4 (`14`).

## 1. Central question (from `01` §4, corporate axis)

**Do corporations remain instruments of states or become sovereign powers?**

## 2. The corporate sovereignty ladder (shared mechanic)

A ladder (mirroring the SovOil ladder in `08`) applied to major corp↔state pairs:

1. **State instrument** — corp serves the state.
2. **Chartered actor** — corp has legal autonomy within the state.
3. **Parallel power** — corp co-governs domains (security, energy, Net).
4. **State capture** — corp controls the government.
5. **Sovereign corporation** — corp *is* a geopolitical power (playable as a country tag).

Each corp↔state pair sits somewhere on this ladder and can move via focuses/decisions/events.

## 3. Corp–state pairings (see `04` corporate map)

| Corp | State | Start position | Sovereign-corp end |

| Arasaka | Japan | Parallel power | Sovereign security empire |

Loose-entanglement corps (Petrochem, Biotechnica, Orbital Air, EBM) act mainly through influence, not
the full ladder, unless a path elevates them.

## 4. Mechanics to define

- How a corp becomes a **playable sovereign tag** (release/breakaway mechanic).
- Corporate **influence** over foreign states (feeds `12`).
- Interaction with **union cohesion** (SovOil) and NUSA **federal authority**.
- Corporate **private military** representation (Militech, Arasaka security).
