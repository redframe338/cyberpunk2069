# The Fractured World, 2069

A Hearts of Iron IV total-conversion mod set in the Cyberpunk universe of the late twenty-first century.

## Concept

This is a **global geopolitical total conversion**, not a Night City adaptation. The old
international system survived ecological collapse, corporate wars, economic fragmentation, and the
destruction of the global Net — but emerged fundamentally transformed. States are rebuilding power,
corporations have become geopolitical actors, and the next global conflict may decide whether the
future belongs to national governments, supranational blocs, corporate empires, or autonomous
networks.

**Start date:** 1 January 2069.

The character V does not appear. Night City is one major strategic flashpoint among many, not the
center of the world.

## The four principal power centers

1. **New United States of America (NUSA)** — a recovering continental power reconstructing state
   authority; powerful but internally compromised.
2. **Neo-Soviet Union** — a first-tier superpower with its own institutions, ideologies, military,
   and Eurasian ambitions. **Never** a mere shell of SovOil.
3. **Europe** — a dense political region of competing states, institutions, currencies, and
   corporations, contesting the nature of integration itself.
4. **Arasaka & Japan** — a global corporate actor *and* a sovereign state, held distinct.

Plus **China & Kang Tao** and the **major transnational corporations** as independent power centers.

## Core design rules

- The NUSA, Neo-Soviet Union, Europe, Japan, China, and the major corporations are **independent
  centers of power**. No single North American storyline defines the global campaign.
- The Neo-Soviet Union must **never** be treated as a minor regional faction or as the political
  shell of SovOil. It is a major sovereign superpower.
